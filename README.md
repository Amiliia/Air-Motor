# Air-Motor

Air Motor (AutoCAD, CNC/Lathe/Milling): Designed an air motor in AutoCAD and manufactured all components using lathe, milling, and CNC processes; fully assembled into a working mechanical system. (Jan – April 2025) 

## Overview

Between January and April 2025, I designed and manufactured my own air motor as a hands-on mechanical engineering project.

The project involved taking a complete air motor concept from a **2D CAD design in AutoCAD** to a **fully constructed physical prototype**. Each component was individually designed and manufactured from metal using workshop machining processes, including CNC machining, lathe operations, and milling.

This project allowed me to develop practical experience in mechanical design, precision manufacturing, machining, and the relationship between CAD drawings and real-world component production.

## Project Timeline

**January 2025 – April 2025**

## Project Objectives

- Design an air motor from the ground up.
- Create detailed AutoCAD drawings for each individual component.
- Manufacture the components using metalworking and machining techniques.
- Gain practical experience with CNC, lathe, and milling machines.
- Assemble the manufactured components into a functional air motor.
- Understand the challenges involved in translating technical drawings into physical parts.

## Design & Manufacturing Process

The project followed a design-to-manufacturing workflow:

1. **Concept & Design**
   - Developed the air motor design using AutoCAD.
   - Created individual drawings and dimensions for each component.

2. **Component Manufacturing**
   - Used the technical drawings as references for manufacturing.
   - Produced the components from metal using:
     - CNC machining
     - Lathe machining
     - Milling

3. **Assembly**
   - Assembled the individually manufactured components into the complete air motor.
   - Checked the fit and alignment of the parts during assembly.

4. **Final Prototype**
   - Completed a physical air motor based on the original CAD design.
